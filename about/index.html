Who We Are

Healy's Equipment Rentals is a locally owned small business focused on providing reliable, well‑maintained equipment for contractors, homeowners, and DIY projects. The goal is to make getting the right machine for the job fast, straightforward, and affordable.

What We Offer

  • Compact electric excavator sized for residential and light commercial work.
  • Attachments to support trenching, digging, and site cleanup.
  • Flexible rental options for single‑day, weekend, or multi‑day projects.

How We Work

Rentals are structured to keep the process easy: clear daily rates, flexible timeframes, and straightforward pickup or delivery options where available. Every rental starts with a quick conversation to match equipment to the job, helping customers avoid overspending on the wrong machine.

Service Area & Contact

Healy's Equipment Rentals currently serves customers in and around Mechanicsburg, PA. For questions, availability, or to schedule a rental, use the email and request form on the Rentals page or reach out directly at info@healysequipmentrentals.com.
